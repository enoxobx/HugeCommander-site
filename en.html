The fast, keyboard-first dual-pane file manager

The clean look of Windows 11, the keys of Total Commander, folder and file comparison. For Windows 10 and 11.

Read the guide Microsoft Store (testers)

Panes and tabs

Two to four panes, each with its own tabs, in five layouts (Alt+1…5). Everything is back where you left it at the next start.

Comparison

Whole folder trees, also on the network, text files side by side, three-way merge, and synchronization with a preview of every copy.

Instant search

Finds files on every drive as you type, without administrator rights, and searches the text inside files too (Alt+F7).

Background operations

Copy and move with progress, pause, cancel and retry. Rename many files with a preview, and Ctrl+Z undoes the last operation.

External drives

USB sticks, USB disks and memory cards show up by themselves, with name and free space. Safely remove them with Ctrl+Shift+E or from the Windows taskbar.

Total Commander keys

Your Total Commander shortcuts are imported automatically. Every key can be searched and changed in the legend (F1).

And more: preview of code, images and CSV tables (Ctrl+Q, F3) · zip archives as folders · network shares · Windows context menu and drag and drop · light and dark themes · English and Italian.
